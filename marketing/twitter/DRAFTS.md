## Day 1

### 10:00 AM
> Ever notice how the moment someone asks what you're most proud of this year, your mind goes blank? Not because you didn't do anything — because none of it got written down when it actually happened. By review time it's just a vague feeling the year went fine.
>
> #CareerAdvice #WorkLife

### 2:00 PM
> A friend asked me to look over her resume last month. Every line said "helped with" or "supported." Turns out she'd actually rebuilt a broken handoff process that was costing her team three days a cycle — got it down to four hours. She just never described it that way, so it never looked that way on paper. Same work, completely different resume.
>
> #ResumeTips #CareerAdvice

### 6:00 PM
> The "brag document" advice is everywhere — write your wins down as they happen. Almost everyone starts one at some point. Almost nobody keeps it past week two. Not bad advice, just hard to remember to do in the moment.
>
> #CareerAdvice #BragDocument

## Day 2

### 10:00 AM
> It's not that you're bad at talking about yourself. It's that by the time an interview or review rolls around, the details that made a win actually impressive have already faded into "yeah, that went well I think."
>
> You can't sell a story you can't remember.
>
> #CareerGrowth #CareerAdvice

### 2:00 PM
> When people tell me about a project, they usually start vague — "I helped launch something," "it went okay." So I ask three questions: What did you actually decide or do? What would've happened if you hadn't been there? Is there a number attached? By the third answer, there's usually a real story hiding under the vague one.
>
> #CareerAdvice #ResumeTips

### 6:00 PM
> Every productivity app assumes you want to open it and type. Nobody wants to type after a long day. The stuff worth remembering happens in real time — thirty seconds after it happens, not whenever you finally sit down to journal about it.
>
> #WorkLife #Productivity

## Day 3

### 10:00 AM
> Performance review season hits different when your manager asks what you're proud of this year and you can only really remember the last three weeks.
>
> #CareerAdvice #WorkLife

### 2:00 PM
> Two people did the exact same job. One wrote "Responsible for onboarding" on their resume. The other wrote "Led onboarding revamp, cutting new-hire ramp time by 30%." Same role, same work. Only one of them got called back.
>
> #ResumeTips #JobSearch

### 6:00 PM — question
> What's something genuinely impressive you did at work that never made it onto your resume or your LinkedIn — anywhere? Not because it wasn't real. Just because nobody asked, so you never mentioned it.
>
> Curious what you've got.
>
> #CareerAdvice #WorkLife
