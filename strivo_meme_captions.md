## Posting tips
- Keep the core hashtag block consistent across posts (helps the algorithm associate them with the Strivo account/brand).
- Mix in 3–4 extra tags per post from above so hashtags don't look identical every time.
- Instagram allows up to 30 tags, but 8–12 well-chosen ones tend to perform better than maxing it out.
